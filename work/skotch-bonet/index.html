Skotch Bonet

Restructuring Bash Hesnef's sister agency around a paid 10-day diagnostic sprint for early-stage founders.

ClientSkotch Bonet (sister company of Bash Hesnef)
My roleStrategy lead
Year2026
ContextMaster in Strategic Design thesis, Politecnico di Milano · live venture in Dubai
02The brief

Bash Hesnef designs and fits out workspaces in Dubai. Its clients were spending on the space but arriving without the brand strategy to fill it. Skotch Bonet, a sister company, was set up to capture that value upstream.

Dubai's tech ecosystem attracted a record $5 billion in startup investment (Digital Dubai, 2026), and creative agencies fight over it with hourly billing and free pitches. This thesis is the operating plan for doing it differently.

03The insight

Founders speak growth and runway. Agencies speak colours and fonts. That gap costs startups their seed money.

  1. 42% of startups fail because of a strategy gap: they misread demand and build something nobody wanted (Founders Forum).
  2. Hourly billing drains runway before product–market fit, and subjective design debates replace investor-ready business logic.
  3. How might we restructure the agency model from a provider of intangible services to a strategic partner that integrates business model innovation with bold creativity?
04The concept

A Blue Ocean pivot. Eliminate free pitching and account managers. Reduce the service menu and the taste debates. Raise speed and direct access to senior strategists. Create a paid diagnostic sprint.

  1. Week 1, the autopsy: understand the business, then a red-flag report on why the current brand is failing.
  2. Week 2, the surgery: three directions on day 6 (safe, bold and terrifying), and a working design system handed over on day 10.
  3. Scope creep is designed out: the CEO must be in the room on day 6, work is shown raw early, and revisions after day 10 need a new contract.
  4. Clients come through Bash Hesnef's fit-out pipeline and through VC partners, who get portfolio-wide access.
01The unit economics
01
Freelancer payout is capped at 40% of each sprint fee. The remaining 60% covers software, tax and profit.
02
Fixed costs near zero: no office lease, a core team of two, freelancers paid only when a sprint is funded.
03
Three active retainers cover the first year of operation.
04
Revenue is earned in AED in Dubai; heavy creative execution runs through a team in India.
05The outcome

My final thesis for the Master in Strategic Design at Politecnico di Milano. I own the business logic: the sprint method, the unit economics and the VC relationships. Skotch Bonet is a live prototype now being established in the UAE.
